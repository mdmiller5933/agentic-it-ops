## Color-coded status line

The most reusable format in the corpus - one line, scannable, works in a digest or standalone:

```
<color> <STATUS IN CAPS> | <date> | <scope>
```

```
:red_circle: UPCOMING OUTAGE | Saturday, July 11 | 9 AM - 2 PM | 811 Main
:large_yellow_circle: ROLLING OUT | Monday, July 13 | All computers
:large_blue_circle: ONGOING | Monitor flickering | Fix in progress
```

Progress/phase variant, used for migrations:

```
:white_check_mark: Recently Completed
:large_green_circle: Completed Departments
:large_yellow_circle: In Progress
```

Convention: red = outage/impact, yellow = change landing or in progress, blue = known issue being
worked, green/check = done.
